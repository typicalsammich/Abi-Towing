FAQ

Heavy-Duty Towing Questions, Answered.

Common questions about semi towing, commercial vehicles, RVs, equipment, heavy recovery and long-distance transport.

What vehicles do you handle for heavy-duty towing?

HeavyDuty Towing focuses on semis, larger commercial vehicles, work trucks, motorhomes, RVs, 5th wheels, equipment and other loads that require higher-capacity towing or transport equipment.

When should I call for semi-truck towing?

Call when a tractor, trailer or commercial rig is disabled, damaged, involved in an accident or needs relocation and its size, weight or axle configuration requires heavy-duty equipment.

Can you tow RVs and 5th wheels?

Yes. RV, motorhome, camper, trailer and 5th-wheel towing is available based on the unit's dimensions, weight, hitch setup and condition.

Do you transport containers or equipment?

HeavyDuty Towing advertises transport for select equipment and 20-foot or 40-foot containers. Call with the dimensions, approximate weight, pickup conditions and destination before dispatch.

What information should I have ready when I call?

Provide the vehicle or load type, approximate weight if known, current condition, exact location, destination and any access issues. For semis or trailers, include axle and trailer information when possible.

Can you recover a large vehicle from a ditch or difficult position?

Heavy recovery can include winching, repositioning and accident recovery when the vehicle is stuck, off-road or cannot be loaded normally. The exact setup depends on weight, terrain and access.

How far can HeavyDuty Towing travel?

HeavyDuty Towing advertises long-distance capability up to 500 miles from Fresno for eligible jobs. Availability depends on the vehicle, equipment required and destination.
