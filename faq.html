FAQ

Straight Answers Before The Truck Arrives.

Common questions about towing, roadside assistance, vehicle recovery and long-distance transport.

What's the difference between light-duty and heavy-duty towing?

Light-duty towing is intended for standard passenger vehicles and smaller trucks. Heavy-duty towing uses higher-capacity equipment for large commercial vehicles, semis, construction equipment and other heavy loads.

When should I call for semi-truck towing?

Call for semi-truck towing when the vehicle's size, gross weight, axle configuration or damage exceeds the capacity of a standard flatbed or light-duty tow truck.

Do you offer fuel delivery?

Yes. Roadside assistance includes fuel delivery for drivers who run out of gas, along with services such as jump starts and tire changes.

Can you change a flat tire?

Yes. Roadside tire-change service is available when the vehicle is in a safe enough location to perform the work.

What affects long-distance towing cost?

Mileage, vehicle size and weight, equipment required, recovery complexity and pickup or drop-off access can all affect pricing.

What's the difference between roadside assistance and recovery?

Roadside assistance handles minor issues such as dead batteries, fuel shortages and flats. Recovery is used when a vehicle is stuck, off-road, in a ditch or otherwise needs winching or repositioning before transport.

How far can A's Towing travel?

The existing A's Towing website advertises service within up to 500 miles of Fresno. Availability depends on the specific job, so call with the pickup and destination addresses.
